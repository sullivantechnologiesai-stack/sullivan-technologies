About

Built by a veteran. Operated like the business it's meant to serve.

Sullivan Technologies is veteran-owned and operated, founded and run by Christopher "Sully" Sullivan out of Kitsap County, Washington. The business grew out of a simple observation: small and mid-sized businesses lose a surprising amount of their day to work that doesn't need a human — and most of them have no one looking at it.

I've spent over five years building and running automation — the kind that sits in the background and removes the repetitive work that eats a day. That experience spans enterprise environments across several sectors, where the work was less about pushing a tool and more about looking at a real process, finding the repeat stuff, and building the automation that removed it. The tooling has changed over time, but the shape of the problem hasn't: a business doing something by hand that could be running itself, and nobody looking at it closely enough to fix it. Before Sullivan Technologies, I was the person on the other side of the table doing that kind of work — writing reusable automation, documenting the pieces so they could actually be maintained, and sitting between the technical side and the business side to make sure what got built was useful, not just impressive.

That's the wedge. Not selling a platform, not pushing a tool, not chasing the latest shiny thing. Looking at a business's actual workload, finding the repeat stuff that adds friction, and building the automation that removes it — email, process, systems, or practical AI applied to a real task.

The work is hands-on. Sully is in the room for the first conversation, in the scope, and close to the build. The model is built to scale into a broader automation practice over time — but the starting point is one business, one owner, one real problem, solved.

Who we're built for: small and mid-sized businesses, owners and teams who are busy but not busy in the right way — spending time on the repetitive work that should be automated, not the work that actually needs them.
