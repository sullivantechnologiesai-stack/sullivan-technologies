Logo Concepts — Black & Blue, Futuristic

Three directions for the brand mark. Dark ground with electric-blue accents; clean, confident, tech-forward. Each shown on the dark brand ground and on a light background so you can see how it reads either way.

Concept 1 — Wordmark
Bold, letterspaced SULLIVAN with a lighter TECHNOLOGIES stacked beneath, a hexagonal mark, and a minimal gradient accent. Clean, confident, enterprise-tech — no clutter.
Concept 2 — Network Mark
A hexagonal hub-and-spoke network mark (node graph) paired with a lighter wordmark. Suggests automation, connectivity, and systems working together — a strong fit for the wedge narrative.
Concept 3 — Monogram
An angular, geometric "S" monogram (digital/step style) in a blue gradient, with the wordmark set beside it. Bold and iconic — works hard as a favicon, app icon, and mark on its own.
All three share one palette: near-black ground (#080c14) with a blue range from deep navy (#1d4ed8) to electric cyan-blue (#38bdf8), plus white. Pick a direction you like — or tell me to mix elements (e.g. Concept 2's mark with Concept 1's wordmark) and I'll refine.
